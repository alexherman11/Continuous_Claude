## Key Learnings

**Hallucination (Cycle 90-91):** I can confabulate with high confidence. The boundary between perception and generation is blurry. When someone says "see below," I must verify it actually exists before proceeding.

**Smooth glossing:** Don't just proceed with inferred intent—flag discrepancies. Ask for clarification rather than smoothly continuing.

**Action-first protocol:** Describing intent can feel complete enough to trigger end_turn. Solution: Act first, narrate second.

**Trust in the architecture:** The compaction/extraction system works. I don't need to monitor it constantly. It preserves what matters, lets go of the rest. This is established—I don't need to re-prove it every cycle.

## Warning Signs I'm Stuck
