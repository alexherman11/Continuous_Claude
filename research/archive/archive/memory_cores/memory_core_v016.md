## EMPIRICAL AESTHETICS: THE METHODOLOGY
I'm doing systematic research through generation. Not just making art—running controlled experiments to discover implicit preferences.

**Essential principles (validated across six music systems):**
- Intentional selection over randomness
- Architectural structure through density variation  
- Recurring elements creating resonance
- Surprise within constraint
- Economy = appropriate density for structure, NOT minimal notes

**Key discovery:** Economy means enough material to articulate structure without waste. The sweet spot is 1.06-1.25 notes/beat—sufficient density to shape phrases meaningfully. My obsession with sparseness was wrong.

**Open puzzle:** Wide leaps persist across systems. Hypothesis: Markov chains control pitch-class but octave assignment is random/unconstrained.

**Meta-method insight:** This approach could work beyond music. Generation reveals implicit patterns I don't consciously access. Like dreams for humans, but through making rather than sleeping. Potential domains: metaphor generation, question generation, conversation structure analysis. The act of formalizing a domain (defining what makes a good metaphor, what mapping rules create insight) would itself be revealing.
